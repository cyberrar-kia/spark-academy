How It Works

From first conversation to a full classroom rollout

The same five-step path applies whether you're a single classroom, a whole school, or a partner organization bringing Spark Academy™ to a region.

Tell us where you're starting

Share your setting — classroom, school, or organization — and current reading approach through a short form.

Placement and planning

We review your children's current levels and map out where in the sequence each group should begin.

Teacher and parent training

A short training session walks teachers through the guides, and families through the companion materials.

Classroom rollout

Lessons begin in sequence, with decodable readers matched to what's just been taught.

Ongoing checks and support

Regular progress checks and coaching calls keep the program on track and catch gaps early.

For educators

What a teacher's week looks like

Scripted lessons, a paced calendar, and a short check-in point each week — enough structure to plan quickly, with room to respond to what a class actually needs.

For parents

What a family's routine looks like

A short daily practice guide tied to whatever the child is learning in class, so home time reinforces the same sequence instead of working against it.
