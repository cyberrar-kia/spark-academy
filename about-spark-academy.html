About Spark Academy™

A structured path from first sounds to independent reading

Spark Academy™ is a sequenced literacy curriculum, built so a teacher, tutor, or parent always knows exactly what a child is ready to learn next.

The method

Small steps, in the right order

Rather than a general reading program, Spark Academy™ sequences specific skills — sounds, blends, sight patterns, comprehension strategies — so nothing is introduced before its foundation is in place.
